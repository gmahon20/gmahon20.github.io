How we review

We prioritize useful, accurate information over hype. Our guides describe relevant features, possible drawbacks, and questions to ask before purchasing.

When hands-on testing is available, we'll clearly say what we tested and how. Otherwise, our articles are labeled research-based buying guides.

Our editorial checklist

  1. Confirm product identity and specifications from reliable sources.
  2. Compare real differences, not invented performance claims.
  3. Identify who may or may not benefit from the product.
  4. Label affiliate links and paid partnerships.
  5. Recheck prices, availability, and claims before publication.

Adjustable Laptop Stands: Buying Guide

Research-based guide; we have not personally tested products.

What to compare

Look for height adjustment, laptop size compatibility, ventilation, grip and stability. Check the manufacturer's weight limits and return terms.

Tradeoffs

Foldable stands are portable, but some can wobble when typing. Raised stands may work best with an external keyboard.

# TrendVault Reviews affiliate link report

Checked October 23, 2026. Tracking ID: `trendvaultrev-20`.

Scope: 25 priority guides plus the three original launch guides (28 total). The launch guides were linked on the homepage but absent from the repository; they have been restored.

Test method: request each full affiliate URL, follow redirects, confirm HTTP 200, inspect the product title and ASIN, and check that Add to Cart and Buy Now controls are present. No purchase or cart action was performed. A passing result verifies the product destination and visible purchase controls at the check time; it does not guarantee future stock, delivery to every address, or commission attribution.

Each link uses a specific Amazon product detail page, not an Amazon search page. The exact tracking tag is checked in both the requested URL and final URL. Product identity was reviewed against the guide topic.

Amazon account approval, registered website status, cookies and actual commission credit require the account’s Associates dashboard and cannot be established from public links.

Post-publication check: **28/28 guide pages and their Amazon destinations passed**. URLs were extracted from the live GitHub Pages HTML and requested again.

## Editorial and disclosure changes

Preserved the homepage’s colorful design, six category groups and capitalized navigation. Added product-specific summaries and fit limitations. Removed obsolete statements claiming that pages had no affiliate links. Added the required Amazon Associate statement beside each paid link; purchase buttons are explicitly labeled paid links and use sponsored/nofollow/noopener attributes. No Amazon prices, star ratings or product photos were copied. No hands-on testing is claimed.

The old fixed-height laptop stand was replaced with an adjustable model, the unavailable phone-tripod bundle with a standalone tripod, and the unavailable Belkin hub with an Anker hub. Unavailable Copco 9-inch and WAVELU liner candidates were rejected in favor of the listed Copco 12-inch turntable and HIC liner.

ChomChom appears in both the reusable lint roller and pet hair remover guides because the same product fits both topics. These are separate guide links, not two different products.
